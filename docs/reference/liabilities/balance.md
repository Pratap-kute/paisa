# Liability balances

The Liability Balance view shows outstanding loans, debts, and credit lines as
an account hierarchy. For each liability account, Paisa can show the amount
drawn, principal repaid, interest recorded, and the remaining balance.

The figures come from postings in your journal. If interest and principal are
not recorded separately, Paisa cannot provide the same breakdown. Use
[Interest](interest.md) for rates and interest timelines, or
[Repayments](repayment.md) for monthly debt reduction.
