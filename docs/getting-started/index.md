# Getting started

Paisa turns a plain-text accounting journal into useful reports for spending,
investments, liabilities, and financial plans.

1. [Install Paisa](installation.md) as a desktop app, command-line tool, or Docker container.
2. Follow [First setup](tutorial.md) to configure Paisa and add your first transactions.
3. Use the [journal examples](journal-examples.md) as a starting point for your currency.
4. When you are ready, [import a statement](../reference/import.md) or edit the journal directly.

!!! tip "Start small"

    You do not need years of transaction history on day one. A recent opening
    balance, income, regular expenses, and investments are enough to start
    learning from your data.
